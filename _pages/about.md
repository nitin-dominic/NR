### **Short Bio and Research Vision**

As an agricultural engineer, I develop and deploy autonomous robotic systems for site-specific crop management. My work centers on field robotics, integrating ground and aerial platforms with onboard sensing, embodied AI, and closed-loop actuation to perform tasks such as targeted spraying, crop monitoring, and in-field phenotyping with minimal human intervention.

With over six years of experience in precision agriculture, I specialize in the design and field evaluation of cyber-physical agricultural systems, from sensor-integrated autonomous platforms to edge-deployed perception models that enable real-time decision-making in production environments. My research spans the full autonomy pipeline: in-field data acquisition using unmanned ground and aerial vehicles (UGVs and UAVs), development of robust perception and localization algorithms, and their deployment on embedded hardware for on-farm operations in both row and specialty crops.

I believe the future of agriculture is fundamentally data-driven. However, the true value of data lies in how effectively we manage, process, and interpret it to generate actionable insights for farmers, growers, stakeholders, agricultural technology providers, and extension educators.
