### Our Mission

We design, build, and field-test autonomous robotic systems that make 
agriculture more precise, sustainable, and labor-resilient. Our work spans 
the full autonomy pipeline — from sensor-integrated ground and aerial 
platforms to edge-deployed perception and closed-loop actuation in 
production fields.

### Research Thrusts

<div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(280px, 1fr)); gap: 1.5rem; margin: 1.5rem 0;">

  <div style="padding: 1.2rem; border: 1px solid var(--global-divider-color); border-radius: 8px;">
    <h4><i class="fas fa-robot"></i> Field Robotics</h4>
    <p style="font-size: 0.9rem;">Autonomous ground and aerial platforms for 
    crop scouting, targeted spraying, and mechanical weeding.</p>
  </div>

  <div style="padding: 1.2rem; border: 1px solid var(--global-divider-color); border-radius: 8px;">
    <h4><i class="fas fa-brain"></i> Embodied AI</h4>
    <p style="font-size: 0.9rem;">Machine perception, motion planning, and 
    edge-deployed inference for real-time decision-making on robotic platforms.</p>
  </div>

  <div style="padding: 1.2rem; border: 1px solid var(--global-divider-color); border-radius: 8px;">
    <h4><i class="fas fa-microchip"></i> Cyber-Physical Systems</h4>
    <p style="font-size: 0.9rem;">Sensing, localization, and closed-loop control 
    for autonomous navigation in unstructured agricultural environments.</p>
  </div>

  <div style="padding: 1.2rem; border: 1px solid var(--global-divider-color); border-radius: 8px;">
    <h4><i class="fas fa-seedling"></i> Phenotyping & Sensing</h4>
    <p style="font-size: 0.9rem;">High-throughput phenotyping using multi-sensor 
    fusion, LiDAR, and hyperspectral imaging for crop assessment.</p>
  </div>

</div>
